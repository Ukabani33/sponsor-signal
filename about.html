Methodology

What's tracked

Sponsor Signal records disclosed brand sponsorships of creators: a brand, a creator, a format (integration, sponsored sketch, podcast read, partner season), a date and a public source link. One row = one placement. The tech-creator vertical currently covers 12 channels and podcasts with developer, engineer and founder audiences, May 2025 – Oct 20, 2026.

Where the data comes from

  • Public YouTube video and podcast descriptions (sponsor lines, “Sponsors:” sections, partner links).
  • Creators' own sponsor pages and public media kits.
  • Paid-partnership tags in public Instagram captions (e.g. #ad, #brandpartner), via public mirrors where noted.
  • A small number of rows from third-party sponsorship trackers — these are flagged unverified.
  • Funding stage comes from the brand's own announcement or reputable press, linked on each brand page. Brands without a checked round show Not checked.

What's not here

  • No personal contact data. No emails, phone numbers or private profiles of anyone — brand-side or creator-side. We link to public pages only.
  • No deal values. Prices aren't disclosed publicly; rate benchmarks on the pitch board are third-party estimates and labelled as such.
  • Undisclosed or private deals.

Caveats

  • Coverage is a sample of high-signal creators, not every channel.
  • Views are as of the data pull and go stale.
  • Dates marked ≈ are approximate (e.g. “8 months ago” on a mirror).

Corrections

Spot something wrong or missing? Each row's source link is the record of truth; corrections are applied at the next weekly rebuild.
