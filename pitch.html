Rate benchmarks

  • Tech YouTube sponsorship CPM: typically $25–$60, so 100K views ≈ $2.5K–$6K. Based on 487 creators, recomputed Jun 2026 (Creatric).
  • Integrations vs. dedicated videos: about $25–$60 CPM for integrations and about $40–$80 for dedicated videos (OutlierKit). A 60-second integration runs roughly 40–55% of the dedicated rate (Grow Creator). I didn't open these two pages; the figures come from search summaries.
  • Instagram macro tier (100K–500K followers): about $5K–$15K per Reel; whitelisting and exclusivity are billed separately (Tally).
  • Lenny's Podcast: no official rate card. Third-party estimates are about $25–$45 CPM, and MyCoCreator estimates about $3.8K per 60-second read (Castlytics, MyCoCreator, SponsorRadar).
  • Theo (t3.gg): says he gets 3M+ views a month, that 76% of his audience is over 25, and that 40+ brands have sponsored him. Pricing is by email only (soydev.link/sponsor-me).
  • What this means for deal size (my arithmetic): at a $25–$60 CPM, one Kai video at ~490K median views is roughly $12K–$30K. Reaching Stareable's $50K–$500K deal size takes a multi-episode series across platforms (YouTube long-form, Shorts and IG), plus usage or whitelisting rights. That's the case for pitching series rather than reads.
